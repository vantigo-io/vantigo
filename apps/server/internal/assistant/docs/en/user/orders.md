The Orders app is where you make quotes for your customers and take their orders. In
Norwegian the app is **Ordre**. Its sidebar has **Quotes**, **Sales orders**,
**Quote templates** and **Orders settings**; the last two need the permission to manage
the Orders settings.

The app is being built. Its screens are not in use yet, and this page describes them as
they arrive.
