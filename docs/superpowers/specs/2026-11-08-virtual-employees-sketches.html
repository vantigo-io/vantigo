Review queue

1 One place to approve

V20Every draft and proposed change of every agent the reviewer may approve. It is the Assistant's pending action, with a supervisor confirming later instead of the person confirming at once.

2 Why it is here

V18Each item says why it waits: the mode, a hard rule, or a score under X. It also shows the sources the answer rests on.

3 The window

V22A Messenger draft shows when its 24-hour window closes. A draft that outlives it is withdrawn and handed off. An agent never uses Meta's human-agent window.

4 Discard teaches

V28The reason on Discard, and edits on Edit and send, become suggestions for the role's instructions (delivery B).
