# Entra phase 1, delivery D — groups and traits: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** implement this plan task by task, one implementer per task and a reviewer after it, never in the same context. Task 4 gets a security-focused reviewer (writes to a tenant's groups through an application credential, and member removals where one wrong path deletes the user). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. No agent commits; the coordinator commits per task group.

**Goal:** groups in Staff and in the tenant. HR keeps **groups** in Staff — listed (HR names the members) or derived (a rule over the register: everyone employed, a department and optionally those beneath it, a business unit, a location, an employee type, a manager's direct reports) — with owners, and the accounts worker materialises a derived group's members on the business day, each change an event on the person. A provider in the slot **declares traits** a group may carry; Staff renders them as a form from the declaration alone, stores the values, and when HR turns the provider on for a group the worker creates the group in the directory with its owners, keeps its name, description and traits in step, and reconciles its members to the people in it who have an enabled account there. For Entra the traits are the group's type, mail nickname, visibility, description, team and licences; the **licences a person gets** are the union of their groups' licence traits, which Staff computes into the spec on every activation and update and the module assigns and strips as direct licences. An archived group, or one HR turned the provider off for, is deleted in the tenant after Staff's `deleteExternalAfterDays`, or renamed `… (archived)` and kept when the setting is never. The leaver's `managed` step, declared in C, now means the module's own groups.

**Architecture:** no new module. Staff gains four tables under `staff` (`00062_staff_groups.sql`), the group endpoints, the rule evaluator, the materialisation and the group sync in the accounts worker's cycle, and the groups on the Account tab and My page; the contract gains a context on `GroupTraits`, a field error type and a length on a trait; the fake provider in `modtest` follows. Entra gains `entra.groups` and two columns on the connection (`00063_entra_groups.sql`), fills `EnsureGroup`, `SetMembers` and `DeleteGroup` against the fake Graph the module already has, reconciles licences on `Update`, reads the service principal's id from its own token, caches the tenant's SKUs on the connection row so the licences trait can be declared with no network, and lists its groups to `entra:manage`. The apps: Staff's **Groups** page and the group page with the per-provider trait form; the module page's **Groups** table. Branch `feat/entra-groups`, cut from `main` at `f4381314` (C merged), the PR against `main`. Two migrations, one per schema, pinned by their tests.

## Readings of the spec

1. **`GroupTraits` takes a context and may fail.** D10's licences trait lists the tenant's SKUs with their counts; a declaration without a context could read nothing. The provider reads them from its own row — the SKUs the last connection test or skus read cached — never the network, as `Provider(ctx)` reads its status; the contract's B signature changes and the fake follows.
2. **The service principal's object id is the token's `oid` claim**: an app-only token names the application's service principal in the tenant, so naming it as an owner needs no `Application.Read.All` and no further permission; it is cached on the connection.
3. **Validation in two places**: the declaration's static rules (required, choices, types, lengths) at the PUT, so HR sees them at once; the provider's own rules (`trait_immutable`, `trait_needs_owner`, a trait another trait forbids) from `EnsureGroup` as a typed field error kept on the row and shown beside the field, not retried until the values change. D10's "the page shows beside the field" is met without the request reaching the network.
4. **The rule's reference**: `id` is a number for a department, a unit, a location and a manager (a person); an employee type is a `value` string; `includeBelow` belongs to the department tree alone, and `reportsTo` is one level as D9 says.
5. **Off and archived share D14's fate**, since D10 says "off deletes or keeps per D14": after Staff's `deleteExternalAfterDays` the external group is deleted; when the setting is never, it is renamed once and kept, its members as they stood, and the row reads `retired`. The rename is Staff's doing through `EnsureGroup` with the suffixed name — the provider knows no archive — so one code path writes names.
6. **Licences are reconciled on `Update` too**, as D10 says and C left out: the missing assigned, the direct ones not in the list removed, a group-assigned one untouched; `Activate` uses the same function.
7. **A pending team is a pending fact, not an error**: `ExternalGroup.Pending` names what the provider could not do yet (`team` on a group younger than fifteen minutes), and Staff leaves the values' hash unwritten so the next cycle asks again; a `GroupTraitError` is for what will not happen until HR changes something.
8. **The person's groups ride on the Account tab's response** (`StaffPersonAccount.groups`), which the person, `staff:manage` and `staff:personal-data` already read, and My page reads its own `GET /me/groups`.
9. **An erased or deleted person leaves every group without an event** — the rows go with the person in the same transaction, and the tenant follows at the next sync through the members' hash.
10. **Groups are not audited** beyond the person events and the provider's trail, as the structure's writes are not.
