> **For agentic workers:** implement this plan task by task, one implementer per task and a reviewer after it, never in the same context. Task 2 lands first, alone; 3a, 3b and 4 run in parallel against it, each owning its files; 5 after 3 and 4; 6 last. Nobody commits but the coordinator.

## Readings this plan takes (into D §8 as 7+ by Task 5)

7. **`photoUpdatedAt` is the cache key**: the apps fetch `GET /people/{id}/photo?v=<updatedAt>`; the server ignores `v` and answers `Cache-Control: private, max-age=300`.
8. **The photo's event payload carries the content type only** (`{"fields": {"photo": {"to": "image/jpeg"}}}`, `photo_removed` with `from`), class D, in the Work group — the person's history names that a photo was set, never the bytes.
9. **The glance's `absence.now`/`next` reuse the absence row's shaping**: a colleague gets `shownAs: absent` with dates, approved or registered rows only; a manager gets the type; an hr_only type is `leave` to all but Self and `staff:personal-data`, exactly as `GET /absences`.
10. **Hours are the user's across every project**, whatever the caller's project rights: the figure is the week's total, no per-project split, no entries.
11. **No new Time permission or endpoint**: Time's provider is a pool read; Time's own screens and rules are untouched.

## The order

Task 2 alone (the contract, the slot, Time's provider, the migration, the queries, the OpenAPI, the shaping group, the stubs). Then 3a (the photo), 3b (the glance and the hours) and 4 (the apps) in parallel. 5 (docs) after 3 and 4, with the two reviews. 6 last.
