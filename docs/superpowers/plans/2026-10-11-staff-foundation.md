# Staff phase 1, delivery A1 — People and employments: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** implement this plan task by task, one implementer per task and a reviewer after it, never in the same context. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. This revision carries the pre-flight of 2026-10-11 (four blockers, ten major, fifteen minor — all taken; its answers are in "The pre-flight's answers").

**Goal:** A new `staff` module in which HR keeps the company's people — identity, contact, next of kin, bank, the sealed national id — their employments as Norwegian arbeidsforhold with dated terms carrying the a-melding and § 14-6 fields, the company's structure, a self-service link to the person's user, and a history of every change; with its own app (People, the person page, Settings, My page's profile), registered everywhere a module is, and documented.

**Architecture:** A standalone module in the Expenses mould: directory `apps/server/internal/staff` (package `staff`), schema `staff`, URL `/api/v1/staff`, contract `openapi/staff.yaml`, frontend package `apps/staff/frontend` (`@vantigo/staff-ui`). It depends on identity only — `Deps.Users` for names and the user picker, `Deps.SMTPSend` for one mail — and reads no other module. One migration for this delivery, `000NN_staff_foundation.sql`, with every column of `staff.people` and `staff.settings` the spec's §3 names (later deliveries add tables and columns to *other* tables — B adds `employments.holiday_scheme_id` — never to these two), plus the A1 tables. The sealed identifiers use a new `secrets.Box.MAC`. Documents, import and export, the payroll CSV, retention, stats and attention (A2), absence (B) and the accounts worker with the managed link (C) are NOT in this plan — but the schema and the contract must not paint them into a corner.

**Tech stack:** Go (pgx v5, sqlc, goose, oapi-codegen strict server), PostgreSQL, React 19, Mantine, TanStack Router + Query, Vitest, Bun.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-staff-foundation-design.md` (revision 3, the pre-flight's amendments marked) — delivery A1: §1, §2 S1–S7, S17, S21, S28, S29; §3.1–3.4, 3.6 (`identity_links` as the self-service link, events, settings); §4 (Days and the zone, Terms, Managers, Sealed fields, Locking); §5; §6 (Meta, People, Employments, Structure, Settings' A1 fields, Me); §7 (My page's profile, People, the Person page's Profile, Employments and History tabs, Settings' structure and A1 fields, Host); §8.2, 8.5, 8.6, 8.7; §9 and §10 (the A1 items); §11. Research: `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-11-staff-module.md` §4.1, §4.7, §4.9, §7.

### Task 1: The platform's MAC, and the identifier package (S4; parallel with Task 2)

`secrets.Box.MAC(purpose string, data []byte) ([]byte, error)` — HMAC-SHA256 under `keyFor(purpose)` (the same HKDF derivation as `Seal`; a distinct purpose string gives a distinct key, so a MAC key never equals a sealing key; the error is `keyFor`'s); `secrets.MACEqual(a, b []byte) bool` constant-time; `Format`/`LogValue` unchanged. `internal/staff/ident` is a stdlib-only leaf **ported from** `customers/values.go:148-215` and `invoices/bankfile/account.go:8-55` with their tests (reading 7): `ParseNationalID(kind, country, raw) (Normalised, error)` — fødselsnummer and d-nummer: 11 digits, the d-nummer's first digit raised by 4, both control digits (weights `3 7 6 1 8 9 4 5 2` and `5 4 3 2 7 6 5 4 3 2`), the century from the individual number (so 29 February is judged in the right year), the month + 40 (H-numbers and Dolly's synthetic persons) and month + 80 (Skatteetaten's synthetic test data) ranges accepted (spec reading 24), `BirthDate()` exposed for the agreement check; foreign: 1–40 chars `[A-Za-z0-9-]` with an alpha-2 country, upper-cased; `IndexInput(n) []byte` — `kind | country | normalised`, the domain-separated MAC input; `ValidateBankAccount(kind, raw)` — BBAN 11 digits MOD11 (weights `5 4 3 2 7 6 5 4 3 2`, the remainder-10 case refused), NO IBAN (15 chars, mod-97 **and** the embedded BBAN's MOD11), any other IBAN by structure (two letters, two digits, 15–34 alphanumerics) and mod-97 — no per-country length table (pre-flight answer 5); `Last4`; `ValidateOrgNumber` (9 digits MOD11, weights `3 2 7 6 5 4 3 2`).

## Readings of the spec

Each is a choice the spec leaves open, or a place where two of its passages cannot both be built, decided here for the user's verdict. **(pre-flight)** marks what the pre-flight settled.

1. **(pre-flight) `identity_links` lands in A1 as the self-service link** — it establishes the Self and Manager audiences and My page (S17), nothing else; HR links any active existing user, Owners included; C's worker acts only on links whose user identity reports managed. The spec's §1, §3.6, S24 and reading 20 were amended accordingly.
2. **The settings contract grows with the deliveries**: A1's `PUT /settings` accepts only the five A1 fields; the absence, account and retention columns exist from A1's migration but reach the contract with B, C and A2.
3. **The employee number is generated as `prefix + next` without padding**, skipping forward past a number HR assigned by hand; a company wanting `EMP007` sets the prefix to `EMP00` while that lasts.
4. **`display_name` is derived once at creation and then HR's to edit**; the person never edits it (§5's Self has ✓ on the Work group; in C a change renames the account).
5. **`DELETE /people/{id}` is a hard delete of a person created in error** — no employment, no link, named as nobody's manager — cascading next of kin, roles and the person's own events, recorded as an installation event with the employee number only; anything else is `person_in_use`, and the retention run (A2) is the only other way a person's data leaves.
6. **(pre-flight) A manager's visibility in A1 covers the work group and every non-pay term of their reports**, as §5 says, reachable through `/people/{id}` and `/org` (My team is B's); the audience is resolved from the terms in force today.
7. **(pre-flight) The identifier checks live in `staff/ident`, ported from customers' and invoices' validators**, not promoted to a platform package: promoting would refactor two modules for a validator three lines long each; a platform `internal/norid` is a later consolidation if a fourth module needs it. `platform-packages.md` names `internal/secrets` only.
8. **(pre-flight) The contract check's item n (tariff)** is `not_applicable` when empty and the employment is not hired out, since many SMBs have no tariff agreement; the check is informative, never blocking.
9. **(pre-flight) `GET /assignable-users` is a Staff endpoint** wrapping `Deps.Users.SearchUsers`, capped at 20, `staff:manage`, named after customers' precedent.
10. **The event payload's shaping is per field by the §5 group table** (the spec's S21 as amended); `payload_classes` serves the erase only.
11. **The mail on a bank-account change goes to the private email when set**, else the work email, skipped with a log line when the person has neither or the driver is not SMTP; it carries at most the last four digits.
12. **The structure's `is_default` is exactly one per table, the first row becomes it, and archiving it is refused** (`structure_default`).
13. **The migration number is decided when Task 2 lands** (`00042` today).
14. **(pre-flight) `other_shift` defaults to 37.5 hours** with no check, since the a-melding leaves it free; `not_shift` likewise.
15. **(pre-flight) `GET /people` for `staff:view` lists people active today only** (data minimisation: an unannounced hire or a leaver stays out of the colleague directory); `staff:manage` gets the `status` filter defaulting to `current` so a half-finished two-step creation stays findable.
