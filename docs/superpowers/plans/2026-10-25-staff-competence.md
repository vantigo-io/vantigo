# Staff phase 2, delivery A — the catalogue and the person's competences: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** implement this plan task by task, one implementer per task and a reviewer after it, never in the same context. Task 2 lands first, alone; 3a, 3b and 4 run in parallel against it, each owning its files; 5 after 3 and 4; 6 last. Nobody commits but the coordinator.

**Goal:** HR keeps a flat catalogue of competences — skills with a level, certificates with a validity, the Norwegian certificates seeded — and a person's competences are declared on My page, verified by their manager, carry their evidence in the archive, are renewed as new rows and are mailed and shown ahead of expiry; Staffing can read who holds what, valid on a date, through `contracts.Workforce`.

**Spec:** [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-staff-competence-design.md`](../specs/2026-10-25-staff-competence-design.md) ("D") S1–S7, S15–S19, §3.1–3.3, §4, §5, §6 (A), §7, §8, readings 1–5, 9–14; the discussion note [`2026-10-25-staff-competence.md`](../research/2026-10-25-staff-competence.md) ("N").

**Pattern code:** `st/document_categories.go` and `st/absence_types.go` (a catalogue with seeds, `seed_immutable`, `If-Match`); `st/next_of_kin.go:125-191` (the canonical person-scoped write under `withPersonLock`); `st/documents.go:275-369` and `st/documents_validation.go` (the multipart upload, `readDocumentForm`, `parseUpload`, `documentObjectKey`, `objectPut`, `removeObject`); `st/accounts_worker.go` and `st/retention_worker.go` (a worker: `Name`, `Interval`, `Run`, `RunCycle`, the `workers` func, `Config.Staff*Enabled`); `st/notifications.go` (mails: plain strings per language, `smtpSend` after commit under one budget, `peopleMails`, `managerDeciders`); `st/attention.go` and `st/accounts_attention.go` (items per caller from the read snapshot); `st/personal_data.go` (a section in both exports); `st/retention.go:57-212` `columnClasses` (a per-person table joins the D erase by being classed); `st/calendar_source.go` (a provider that is its own gatekeeper, rule 12); `module/compose.go:190-218` (a sole-provider slot); `st/shaping.go` (field groups, `reads`, `personResponse` capabilities); `ui/pages/-document-categories.tsx` and `-absence-types.tsx` (catalogue cards with archive and hide), `ui/components/document-upload.tsx` (the file picker with the camera on a phone), `ui/pages/-my-team.tsx` (`FieldGroup` cards).

`srv/` is `apps/server/internal/`, `st/` `srv/staff/`, `mig/` `srv/db/migrations/`, `ui/` `apps/staff/frontend/src/`, `host/` `apps/host/frontend/src/`, `R/` `docs/src/content/docs/en/reference/`, `U/` `docs/src/content/docs/{en,nb}/user/`, `C/` `docs/src/content/docs/en/contributing/`.

## Readings of the spec

1. **Settings has no tabs** (`ui/pages/settings.tsx` is a stack of cards): the "Competence settings" are two cards after Document categories, not a tab; D §7 is read accordingly.
2. **No new erase kind.** D S17 names kinds `competences` etc.; the existing erase reports one kind `D` for every whole-D table it deletes (`next_of_kin` is the precedent), and the evidence falls under the documents' erase. The event and the response are unchanged; the spec is amended at revision 2.
3. **Row operations live at `/person-competences/{id}`**, as `/documents/{id}` and `/absences/{id}` do, with the list and the creation under `/people/{id}/competences`.
4. **The stamp precedes the send** (D §9's "mail server down"): the worker stamps the row in a transaction, closes it, then mails; a send that fails is logged, never retried, and the dashboard item is the durable signal (D reading 5).
5. **A manager's or HR's add is verified at once** with basis `observed`, since they vouch for it by entering it; the person's declaration is not.
6. **`canVerifyCompetence` needs no I/O**: it is audience-derived, unlike `canDecide`.
7. **`Workforce` is a sole-provider slot**, not a many-provider one: Staff is the one register.
8. **Expired rows raise an item but are still "held" by nothing**: `HeldCompetences` applies the validity window, so an expired certificate is absent from it on today's date.
9. **The person page shows the tab to every audience that sees the person**, since the Colleague reads the Competence group — unlike Absence, which a Colleague reads as dates only through a different endpoint.
